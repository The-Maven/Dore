Cuba Designations and Designations Updates; Issuance of Russia-related General License

 
 

 
 
 

 

 
 
 

 

 
Release Date
 
05/18/2026
 

 

 
Recent Actions Body
 

The U.S. Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) is issuing Russia-related General License 134C, "Authorizing the Delivery and Sale of Crude Oil and Petroleum Products of Russian Federation Origin Loaded on Vessels as of April 17, 2026."
Additionally, OFAC has updated the Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List.

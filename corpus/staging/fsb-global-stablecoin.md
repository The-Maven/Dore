# FSB — High-level Recommendations for the Regulation, Supervision and Oversight of Global Stablecoin Arrangements (Final report, July 2023)

The Financial Stability Board's international baseline. Ten recommendations
national regimes are expected to build on. Source:
https://www.fsb.org/2023/07/high-level-recommendations-for-the-regulation-supervision-and-oversight-of-global-stablecoin-arrangements-final-report/

## Scope and objectives

The FSB's recommendations aim to establish consistent regulation,
supervision and oversight of global stablecoin arrangements (GSCs) across
jurisdictions while addressing financial stability risks and supporting
responsible innovation. They apply to arrangements that combine multiple
functions (issuance, transfer, custody, stabilisation, redemption) and have
the potential for cross-jurisdictional reach.

## Recommendation 1 — Authorities' readiness

Authorities must possess appropriate powers, tools and resources to
comprehensively regulate, supervise and oversee GSC arrangements and their
functions, and to enforce relevant regulations effectively.

## Recommendation 2 — Comprehensive oversight

Authorities should apply comprehensive and effective regulatory,
supervisory and oversight requirements consistent with international
standards, on a functional basis and proportionate to the risks of each
GSC's activities.

## Recommendation 3 — Cross-border cooperation

Authorities should cooperate internationally and domestically — sharing
information, coordinating supervisory action and ensuring consistent
regulatory oversight across borders and sectors.

## Recommendation 4 — Governance structures

GSC arrangements must establish transparent governance frameworks with
clear and direct lines of responsibility and accountability for all
functions and activities, including across the network of service
providers.

## Recommendation 5 — Risk management

GSC arrangements require robust risk management frameworks addressing
operational resilience, cybersecurity, anti-money laundering /
counter-terrorism financing, and fit-and-proper standards for those who
control key functions.

## Recommendation 6 — Data storage and access

GSC arrangements must implement systems for collecting, storing and
reporting data accurately, with regulatory authorities having the access
necessary to fulfil their mandates.

## Recommendation 7 — Recovery and resolution

Authorities should require that GSC arrangements have appropriate recovery
and resolution plans, so that an arrangement's failure does not transmit
risk to the wider financial system.

## Recommendation 8 — Disclosures

GSC issuers must provide comprehensive, transparent information regarding
governance, conflicts of interest, redemption rights, operations, risk
management and financial condition — including the composition and
custody of the reserve.

## Recommendation 9 — Redemption and stability

GSC arrangements must guarantee timely redemption at par in fiat currency,
maintain stable value through effective stabilisation mechanisms, and meet
prudential requirements appropriate to their function and risk profile.

## Recommendation 10 — Pre-operation compliance

GSC arrangements must meet all applicable regulatory requirements before
commencing operations and adapt to new requirements as supervisory
expectations evolve.

Doré — a doré bar is the semi-pure gold a mine produces before it is refined and assayed to certified purity: raw claimed value that must be verified. The wordmark is set in a precise high-contrast serif; the accented é carries the one note of gold — gilded, doré — and the fine ruled tick reads as an assay gauge, not an icon. By Rayleigh Stark sits quiet and tracked beneath: the parent endorsement, never the hero. No coins, no circuitry — institutional, instrument-grade.
